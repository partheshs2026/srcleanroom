Step-By-Step Workflow

Our 10-Step Execution Process

Step 01

Initial Consultation & Requirement Gathering

Defining air change rates, pressure differentials, and thermal requirements.

Step 02

Site Survey & Feasibility Analysis

Evaluating structural capacity, space availability, and utility routing.

Step 03

Conceptual Design & DQ (Design Qualification)

Drafting HVAC layouts, airflow Schematics, and wall panel configurations.

Step 04

Engineering & Equipment Selection

Sizing AHUs, chillers, filtration levels, and automatic BMS controls.

Step 05

Procurement & Quality Checks

Sourcing certified modular panels, HEPA units, and duct materials.

Step 06

Installation & Erection

Erecting modular panels, duct fitting, electrical cabling, and piping setup.

Step 07

System Balancing (TAB)

Air balancing, adjusting dampers, and establishing cleanroom pressure cascades.

Step 08

Validation (IQ/OQ/PQ)

Particle counting, filter integrity test, air velocity, and recovery testing.

Step 09

Handover & Client Training

Handing over compliance documentation, drawings, and operational training.

Step 10

Ongoing AMC Support

Providing routine inspections, maintenance, and periodic re-validation.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What cleanroom classifications do you construct?
We design and build cleanroom facilities conforming to ISO 14644-1 standards ranging from ISO Class 5 (Class 100) down to ISO Class 9, complete with pressure differentials and environmental controls.
Do you provide full validation reports for audit requirements?
Yes, we deliver complete qualification documentation including Design Qualification (DQ), Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ).
What is included in your Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)?
Our AMC packages include scheduled HEPA filter integrity checks, air flow velocity measurements, differential pressure audits, fan coil & AHU servicing, and priority response for emergency repairs.
